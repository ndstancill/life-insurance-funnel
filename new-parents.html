Questions new parents ask

How much coverage do we need?+
A common starting point is enough to replace several years of income, pay off the mortgage and debts, and set aside money for childcare and education. Many families land somewhere around 10–15× income, but the right number depends on your situation — Dail will walk through it with you.
Does a stay-at-home parent need life insurance?+
Often, yes. If a stay-at-home parent passed away, the family might need to pay for full-time childcare, housekeeping, and transportation while the working parent keeps working. Coverage helps make that possible.
Is it expensive?+
Term life is usually the most affordable type of life insurance, and rates are generally lower the younger and healthier you are when you apply. Your actual price depends on age, health, coverage amount, and term length — a personal quote is the only way to know.
Can I apply while pregnant?+
Many carriers accept applications during pregnancy, but underwriting rules vary by carrier — some may ask about pregnancy complications or ask you to wait until after delivery for certain options. Dail can help find carriers that fit your situation.
We already have coverage through work. Is that enough?+
It's a good start, but employer coverage is often a small multiple of salary and typically ends or changes if you leave the job. Many parents keep it and add a personal term policy on top.
